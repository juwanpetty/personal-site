At the start of every week I create a new note and title it: [Year]: Week [Week #]. I use it as my “what I hope to accomplish that week”. 

I also have a section titled "Interesting Finds" that I use if when I want to look into something later or remember to mention something that I saw online to a friend or family member.

Anything of importance or high priority, I will drop into my Things 3 app.

Inspired by Stephen Ang’s: [How I do my to-dos](https://stephanango.com/todos)
